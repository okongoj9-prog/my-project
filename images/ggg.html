Product details

The SmartPro Cooking Range (Model: SGC-60X60BS) is a versatile 60x60 cm hybrid appliance that combines the efficiency of gas cooking with the precision of electric heating. Its primary feature is a specialized 3+1 cooktop, which consists of three high-performance gas burners and a single 1500W rapid electric hotplate. This configuration offers a helpful backup for different energy sources, ensuring you can continue preparing meals even if you run out of gas. The unit is built with a durable stainless steel tabletop and an elegant black painted body, supported by a sturdy "duck feet" design for enhanced stability and easier cleaning of the floor underneath
